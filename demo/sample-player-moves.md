Sample Player Moves

Scripted moves for replaying the demo. Two players, Thor and Captain America, send these in the room after Natasha's opening message. Each move starts by tagging @natasha and naming the hero.

Replace the hero names if Natasha assigns different roles.

# How to use
Send the kickoff message from kickoff-message.txt.
Wait for Natasha's opening (scene, roles list, Mind Stone Safety 10/10).
Alternate between the two players, sending one move at a time and waiting for Natasha's reply.
Send the insult move (marked below) early so Thanos has something to remember.
When you are done, send @natasha end the game.

Captain America
#	Move
1	@natasha Captain America: I raise my shield and lead the Wakandan soldiers in a charge at the Outriders.
2	Insult: @natasha Captain America: I shout at Thanos, "You may have an army, but you will never get past us!"
3	@natasha Captain America: I throw my shield at the biggest Outrider and catch it on the rebound.
4	@natasha Captain America: I run toward the lab to make sure Vision is safe.
5	@natasha Captain America: I rally everyone and say, "Together, we hold the line, one last push!"

Thor
#	Move
1	@natasha Thor: I call down lightning on the Outriders and clear a path.
2	Insult: @natasha Thor: I shout at Thanos, "You are a coward who hides behind his army!"
3	@natasha Thor: I throw Stormbreaker at Thanos's army and catch it as it flies back.
4	@natasha Thor: I fly to the lab doors and stand guard in front of Vision.
5	@natasha Thor: I raise Stormbreaker and say, "Bring me Thanos. I'm ready to end this!"


Natasha then writes the ending, GAME OVER, and tags Thanos, who answers about the earlier insult.

# Notes
The two insult moves are what set up Thanos's final reply, so keep at least one of them in.
Replies take roughly 10-30 seconds on free models, so trim the pauses when editing the video.
Natasha picks the roles herself, so check her roles list before sending moves and swap hero names to match.
